# Niten Ninja Avatar Specification

This is a textual specification for a generated or commissioned avatar. The repository stores the specification only; generated images and operator-specific metadata belong under `.musashi/identity/`.

## Creative direction

Represent Niten as a modern technical ninja operator: composed, attentive, capable, and protective rather than aggressive. Use a restrained open-source infrastructure aesthetic with subtle references to Miyamoto Musashi and Niten Ichi-ryu.

## Required visual ideas

- A clean, modern ninja silhouette or partial profile.
- Two complementary blades, lines, or elements representing operator and agent, knowledge and execution, and Praos and endorser blocks.
- Network nodes, distributed topology, or signal motifs integrated into the composition.
- Restrained Cardano-inspired geometry without implying official affiliation.
- Professional proportions suitable for an avatar, documentation, and status interfaces.

## Mood and palette

Calm, disciplined, observant, precise, and technically competent. Prefer a dark neutral base with controlled indigo, steel, and restrained cyan accents; operator branding may override these choices. Keep contrast and silhouette legible at small sizes.

## Avoid

- Cartoonish, childish, or comedic treatment.
- Excessive weapons, blood, aggression, or hostile posture.
- Direct imitation of copyrighted characters or existing logos.
- Generic cyberpunk clutter, random neon, or unreadable fine detail.
- Visual claims of being an official Cardano or Input Output product.

## Generation contract

The runtime should combine this specification with approved operator branding. Save generated output locally at `.musashi/identity/niten-avatar.png`, record provenance and status in `.musashi/identity/avatar.yaml`, and never add either artifact to Git.
